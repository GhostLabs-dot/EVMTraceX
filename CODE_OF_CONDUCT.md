# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Ghost Labs welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives. We want participation in EVMTraceX to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical feedback clearly and assume good intent where reasonable.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish private keys, credentials, API tokens, private transaction data, or other sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behaviour are not acceptable. Threats or attempts to expose another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the project's issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing EVMTraceX or Ghost Labs.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainers through GitHub's private contact channels. For security vulnerabilities, use the repository's private vulnerability reporting feature described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as fairly and confidentially as practical and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.
